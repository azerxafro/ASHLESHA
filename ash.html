Level-1 Shichman-Hodges MOSFET real-time simulator (studied at IIT Bombay WEL Lab). Adjust $V_{GS}$ & $V_{th}$ below.

Level-1 Shichman-Hodges Model:
• Cutoff: $V_{GS} < V_{th} \implies I_D = 0$
• Triode / Linear: $V_{DS} < V_{GS} - V_{th} \implies I_D = k_n [(V_{GS}-V_{th})V_{DS} - \frac{V_{DS}^2}{2}](1+\lambda V_{DS})$
• Saturation: $V_{DS} \ge V_{GS} - V_{th} \implies I_D = \frac{k_n}{2}(V_{GS}-V_{th})^2(1+\lambda V_{DS})$
